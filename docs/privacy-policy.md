## Summary

FocusLock does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything the app uses stays on your phone. The app does not request internet permission, contains no ads, no analytics, and has no accounts.

## What is stored on your device

- The list of apps you choose to block
- Your timer settings, study schedules, strict mode setting and daily study goal
- Your focus statistics: daily focus time, completed sessions and number of blocked attempts

This data is saved locally in the app's private storage. It is never uploaded. Uninstalling FocusLock deletes it.

## Permissions and why they are needed

- **Usage access:** lets FocusLock detect which app is in the foreground, only during a focus session or scheduled study time, so it can show the Stay Focused screen when you open an app you blocked. This information is processed on your device and is not stored or shared.
- **Display over other apps:** lets the Stay Focused screen appear on top of a blocked app.
- **Notifications:** shows the "focus mode is on" notice and study-time reminders.
- **Foreground service:** keeps blocking reliable while a session is running.
- **Exact alarms (Alarms & reminders):** starts scheduled blocking and sends reminders at the times you choose.
- **Run at startup:** restores your schedules and any running session after the phone restarts.

## Third parties

FocusLock does not use third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services, and does not share data with anyone.

## Children

FocusLock does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. Because no data leaves the device, no personal information of children is collected.
